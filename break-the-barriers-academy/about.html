About the academy

Your Goals. Our Support.

We don’t just teach English rules — we help you use English in real life.

Who we are

Friendly, practical English training — online.

Break the Barriers Academy is an online spoken-English and communication training academy. Our classes are built around one idea: you improve by speaking.

Sessions focus on practical speaking practice, personalised corrections and pronunciation work, with grammar applied while you speak rather than learned in isolation.

Our focus: helping you speak, improve and grow.
